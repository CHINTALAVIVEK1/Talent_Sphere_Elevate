## **TalentSphere Elevate** 

## **Project Overview** 

Our project is called **TalentSphere Elevate ** 

The main objective of this project is to create one intelligent platform that supports a person's career journey from school, through college, and into professional life. 

Today, students use different websites for career guidance, coding practice, resume building, interview preparation, certifications, and job searching. This makes learning confusing and time-consuming. 

Our goal is to solve this problem by building one platform that provides personalized guidance based on the user's career stage. 

## **Problem Statement** 

Different people have different career needs. 

- High school students need career awareness and guidance. 

- College students need placement preparation and technical skill development. 

- Working professionals need upskilling and career growth support. 

Currently, these services are available on multiple platforms. There is no single intelligent system that brings everything together. 

Our project aims to solve this problem using Artificial Intelligence. 

## **Project Objective** 

We are developing an AI-powered career development platform that can: 

- Identify the user's career stage. 

- Analyze their skills. 

- Find missing skills. 

- Recommend learning resources. 

- Suggest projects and certifications. 

- Track progress. 

- Guide users toward their career goals.

## **System Workflow** 

The workflow is simple. 

1. User registers and logs in. 

2. The user selects their category. 

3. A personalized dashboard is displayed. 

4. The user completes assessments. 

5. AI analyzes the results. 

6. The system identifies strengths and weaknesses. 

7. Personalized learning recommendations are generated. 

8. The user completes learning tasks. 

9. Progress is tracked continuously. 

10. Reports and certificates are generated.
